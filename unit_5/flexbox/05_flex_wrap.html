Example 5: Flex Wrap

Concept:

flex-wrap - Controls whether items wrap to new lines

Default: nowrap (items shrink to fit one line)

flex-wrap: nowrap (Default)

flex-wrap: nowrap;  /* Items stay on one line and shrink */

Items will shrink to fit in one line

Important Note:

align-content only works when there are multiple lines (flex-wrap: wrap)

align-items aligns items within each line

align-content aligns the lines themselves
