Example 4: Variable Hoisting

Concept:

Hoisting is JavaScript's behavior of moving variable and function declarations to the top of their scope during compilation.

⚠️ Only the declaration is hoisted, not the initialization!

Learning Objectives:
1. Understand how var hoisting works
2. Learn about Temporal Dead Zone (TDZ) with let/const
3. See practical differences between var, let, and const hoisting
